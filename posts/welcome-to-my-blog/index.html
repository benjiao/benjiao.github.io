Benjie has a new blog!

February 26, 2026 · Benjie Jiao

I was invited by my old university to give a talk called Learning All the Things last month. Preparing for it made me realize I should probably be better at documenting what I do.

I graduated with a Computer Science degree about 15-ish years ago. Since then, I’ve worked as a software developer and I’ve truly enjoyed it. I like making things.

During the lockdowns of the 2020s, I felt the urge to explore beyond software. I started working on my modular synth project, taught myself electronics, and really enjoyed the process of figuring things out. That project helped me realize I can build things not just for utility, but for fun and self-expression. I also realized I can learn things simply for the sake of learning.

From there, I decided my new goal would be to learn all the things. Woodworking, photography, developing film, birdwatching, homelabbing, 3D printing, CAD, sewing, watercolor… you get the idea.

Along the way, I learned how important it is to share what you make.

So far, I’ve mostly shared notes about my synth-related projects. I’ve been amazed (and grateful) for the community that gathered around them. But what about everything else?

This blog is where I’ll talk about anything that interests me.
